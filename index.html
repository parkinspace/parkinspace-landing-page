The problem

Parking spots sit empty all day, while drivers circle the block.

Your apartment slot is empty from 9 to 6. A theatre lot is packed on Saturday night and deserted on Tuesday morning. Meanwhile, someone down the road can't find a place to park. ParkInSpace connects the two.

People move. Parking doesn't.

Cars shift between homes, offices, theatres and markets through the day, but every spot stays put, so plenty sit empty at any hour.

Empty space, real money

No construction and no new land. Space owners set the hours, the price and the rules, and earn while the space would otherwise be idle.

Less circling, less traffic

Drivers head straight to a confirmed spot instead of hunting for one. Streets get calmer for everyone.
