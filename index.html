The insight

Most parking sits empty for most of the day.

A theatre lot is full on a blockbuster weekend and nearly empty on a Tuesday morning. An apartment slot is empty from 9 to 6. We make that unused time rentable.

Demand changes by the hour

Parking needs move between homes, offices, theatres and markets through the day. The supply just doesn't move with it.

Owners earn from what they already have

No construction and no new land. Space owners set the hours, the price and the rules, and earn while the space would otherwise be idle.

Less street parking, less circling

Vehicle owners get a confirmed slot instead of hunting for a spot. Neighbourhoods get less congestion.
